# Awesome-Field-Workforce-Management

# Top Field Workforce Management Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Scheduling, Dispatch, Mobile Job Execution, Technician Tracking & Field Service Operations*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Field Workforce Management** (also called Field Service Management / FSM). These systems help organizations schedule and dispatch technicians, manage work orders, track mobile teams, capture job data, and optimize field operations.

**Examples** include Skedulo, ServiceMax, WorkWave, Connecteam, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Zuper, FieldPulse, ReachOut, Salesforce Field Service, Workiz, ServiceTitan, and Oracle Field Service (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Full commercial FSM platforms dominate the mid-market and enterprise. The strongest open options are **Odoo Community + OCA Field Service** modules and **ERPNext**-based field-service apps. This section expands those open resources and remains realistic about mobile UX and advanced optimization gaps.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Skedulo](https://www.skedulo.com/)**  
  Modern cloud workforce scheduling and field service platform with constraint-based scheduling, mobile job execution, and strong mid-market adoption in healthcare, utilities, and professional services.

- **[ServiceMax](https://www.servicemax.com/)**  
  Enterprise asset-centric field service platform focused on complex equipment, industrial, and high-tech service operations with deep asset history and governed workflows.

- **[WorkWave](https://www.workwave.com/)**  
  Field service and route management suite used across service industries for scheduling, dispatch, and mobile workforce coordination.

- **[Connecteam](https://connecteam.com/)**  
  All-in-one employee management and field workforce platform popular with deskless teams for scheduling, communication, and task management.

- **[Jobber](https://getjobber.com/)**  
  Leading SMB field service platform for home-service businesses—quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and mobile job management.

- **[Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/)**  
  Popular home-services operating system covering scheduling, payments, marketing, and field execution for trades contractors.

- **[Zuper](https://www.zuper.com/)**  
  Modern field service management platform with intelligent scheduling, mobile apps, and customer experience features.

- **[FieldPulse](https://www.fieldpulse.com/)**  
  Field service software for small-to-mid-size service companies focused on jobs, estimates, invoices, and mobile teams.

- **[ReachOut](https://www.reachoutfs.com/)**  
  Field service management solution for scheduling, dispatch, and mobile workforce visibility.

- **[Salesforce Field Service, Workiz, ServiceTitan, Oracle Field Service and related platforms](https://www.example.com/)**  
  Enterprise and vertical FSM offerings from major vendors—Salesforce Field Service, ServiceTitan (trades), Oracle Field Service, Workiz, and others—covering scheduling, dispatch, and full service lifecycle.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[OCA Field Service (Odoo)](https://github.com/OCA/field-service)**  
  Leading open-source field service modules for Odoo Community—orders, locations, workers, routes, agreements, skills, vehicles, stock, and related FSM workflows (AGPL).

- **[ERPNext / Frappe Field Service apps (e.g. SwiftService, Beveren FSM)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Open-source ERPNext extensions for service requests, engineer dispatch, GPS tracking, spare parts, repair, billing, and job closure.

- **[Odoo Project + Field Service community add-ons](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Core and community modules that combine projects, tasks, inventory, and field service capabilities inside Odoo.

- **[LibreWO and small repair-shop open work-order tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Lightweight open systems for work orders and basic service-shop operations.

- **[Open CMMS / maintenance open platforms (adjacent)](https://github.com/)**  
  Facility and asset maintenance tools that share work-order and technician concepts useful for some field scenarios.

- **[Scheduling and route open libraries](https://github.com/)**  
  Open optimization and routing components that can support custom dispatch engines.

- **[Mobile forms and offline open collectors](https://github.com/)**  
  Self-hosted form and offline-first tools for capturing job data, photos, and signatures in the field.

- **[GPS and location open tracking backends](https://github.com/)**  
  Community location services usable for technician visibility and check-in/out.

- **[Inventory and parts open modules](https://github.com/)**  
  Open stock and parts-consumption components often paired with FSM workflows.

- **[Documentation and FSM open playbooks](https://github.com/)**  
  Guides for configuring Odoo/ERPNext field-service stacks and adapting open tools to service operations.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Deploying **Odoo Community + OCA Field Service** when you need real FSM workflows integrated with CRM, inventory, and accounting.
- Using **ERPNext**-based field-service apps for end-to-end request-to-invoice processes inside an open ERP.
- Combining open work-order tools with mobile forms and simple scheduling for smaller teams.
- Accepting that polished mobile apps, AI dispatch, large-scale optimization, customer portals, and vertical-specific features still favor commercial platforms (Skedulo, ServiceMax, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Salesforce Field Service, Oracle Field Service, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, ERP integration, and lower total cost for mid-size service organizations with technical capacity.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Manage customers and assets in an open ERP (Odoo/ERPNext) → create service orders and schedule technicians with OCA or ERPNext FSM modules → equip field teams with mobile-friendly forms and GPS check-in → consume parts from open inventory → invoice from the same system. Suitable for organizations willing to invest in configuration. Many growth-stage and enterprise service companies still choose commercial FSM platforms for speed of adoption and mobile experience.
